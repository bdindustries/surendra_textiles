Do you sell PU foam in Dibrugarh?

Yes. PU foam is part of our foam range. Share thickness, dimensions, quantity and application so we can discuss a suitable option.

What is HR foam used for?

High-resilience foam is commonly chosen for supportive cushioning where recovery and durability matter, including certain furniture and bedding applications.

Is acoustic foam the same as soundproofing?

No. Acoustic foam is mainly used to manage reflections and room acoustics. Complete sound isolation usually requires a broader construction approach.
