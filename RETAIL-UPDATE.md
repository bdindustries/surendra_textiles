# Retail Outlet Update

This version separates the website's two main customer intents:

- **Dibrugarh retail:** homepage, `store-dibrugarh.html`, and the product pages now clearly present Surendra Textiles as a physical retail outlet in Jhalukpara, Dibrugarh.
- **Assam / Northeast wholesale:** `wholesale-northeast.html` remains focused on bulk, distributor, hotel, institutional and project enquiries.

## Main changes

- Added a dedicated `store-dibrugarh.html` local retail landing page.
- Added **Visit Store** to primary navigation and footer navigation.
- Updated the homepage title, description, H1, hero and store section for local retail intent.
- Added store address, hours, directions, phone and WhatsApp paths prominently.
- Updated mattress, foam, blinds and furnishing pages to welcome retail shoppers while retaining bulk enquiry paths.
- Kept wholesale wording concentrated on the Northeast wholesale page to avoid mixing local retail and regional wholesale search intent.
- Added the retail store page to `sitemap.xml`.
- Added `HomeGoodsStore` structured data for the physical retail outlet without inventing ratings, coordinates or price data.

## Before publishing

Replace every `https://surendratextiles.in` reference in canonical tags, structured data, Open Graph URLs, `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` with the real production domain.
