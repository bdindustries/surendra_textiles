# Product Image Brief — Surendra Textiles

The current build includes the product visuals in `assets/products/`. Keep this brief as the visual reference if any image is regenerated or replaced later.

## Visual direction
Photorealistic Indian commercial product photography; clean premium-but-accessible wholesale catalogue look; natural daylight or soft studio lighting; realistic materials and proportions; no visible third-party brand logos; no text baked into the image; no watermarks; no exaggerated luxury styling; horizontal 4:3 crops with space around the product; consistent neutral warm interiors where applicable.

## Product image set

1. `hotel-mattress-dibrugarh.webp`
   A neatly made hotel room in Northeast India with a high-quality white mattress visible before full bedding is added, neutral upholstered headboard, clean commercial hospitality aesthetic, realistic mattress thickness and edge construction, daylight, photorealistic product photography.

2. `spring-mattress.webp`
   Studio product photograph of a modern spring mattress on a simple low platform, subtle quilted white and light-beige ticking, realistic edges and stitching, neutral background, three-quarter angle, catalogue lighting.

3. `coir-foam-latex-mattress.webp`
   Three unbranded mattress samples displayed side-by-side as realistic cutaway-style product presentations representing coir, foam and latex constructions, clean showroom setting, no labels or text, realistic material textures.

4. `pu-hr-memory-foam.webp`
   Clean stack of foam slabs in different neutral tones and densities representing PU, HR and memory foam, realistic porous textures, warehouse/showroom background softly out of focus, commercial product photo.

5. `acoustic-foam.webp`
   Professional small recording/podcast studio wall fitted with realistic dark acoustic foam panels, desk and microphone softly visible, emphasis on foam texture and room treatment, not claiming full soundproofing.

6. `roller-zebra-blinds.webp`
   Bright modern room showing two adjacent windows, one fitted with a clean roller blind and one with a zebra blind, neutral cream/grey fabric, realistic daylight filtering and installation hardware.

7. `vertical-blinds.webp`
   Office or hotel meeting room with wide glass windows fitted with neat vertical blinds, realistic fabric slats, subtle natural light, commercial interior photography.

8. `hotel-bedsheets.webp`
   Neatly stacked crisp white hotel bedsheets and pillowcases on a clean hospitality linen shelf with a made hotel bed in the soft background, realistic cotton texture, wholesale/hospitality visual.

9. `furnishings-wallpaper-pvc.webp`
   Tasteful home-interior material flat-lay / showroom composition showing wallpaper rolls, a PVC flooring sample, upholstery cloth, dining cloth and doormat, coherent neutral palette, realistic textures, no logos.


10. `sofa-dining-furnishings.webp`
    Warm neutral living-and-dining interior showing sofa upholstery fabrics, cushions, dining cloth and a doormat in a coherent furnishing setting; realistic textile texture; no logos or text.
